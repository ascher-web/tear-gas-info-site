How to read the evidence

  • Established effect Consistent clinical evidence, such as eye irritation, coughing, and skin inflammation.
  • Observed association A link found in studies, such as menstrual changes after exposure. A link does not prove cause.
  • Insufficient evidence Questions research can't answer yet, such as effects on fertility or pregnancy loss.

Research gaps

More research is needed on:

  • Long-term lung health
  • Repeated exposure among civilians
  • Menstrual and hormone effects
  • Pregnancy and fertility outcomes
  • Children's exposure
  • Exposure among nearby residents
  • Differences between chemical agents

Current research shows these chemicals can do more than cause temporary discomfort, but how strong the evidence is depends a lot on which health effect you look at.
